## Short post

Everyone asks "what is my card worth?" Almost nobody asks the harder question: could you actually sell it, and how fast?

So I built a model that scores every Magic card 0 to 100 on liquidity, which is how easily you could turn it into cash today. It looks at the real bid-ask spread (what a dealer pays you versus what they charge), how many shops list it, how often the price actually moves, and how wanted the card is in Commander.

The results match the gut check. Counterspell, the fetch lands, Doubling Season: you can sell those in minutes at a known price. Then there are expensive cards like Pang Tong from Portal Three Kingdoms that look great on paper but almost nobody will buy at a fair price on any given day.

The punchline: a high price tag and an easy sale are not the same thing. A $200 card you cannot move is worth less to you than a $40 card you can sell tonight.

What is the least liquid card in your collection, the one you know would take forever to sell? 👀

## Posting tips

- Lead with the question ("can you actually sell it"), not the math.
- Frame it as a fun tool, not financial advice.
- Have a couple of surprising examples ready (high price, low liquidity) for the comments.
- Check the group's self-promo rules first.
